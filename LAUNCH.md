## LinkedIn

ascii-things 1.0.0 is live.

It is a small library for living ASCII scenes on the web: a night lagoon,
sharks, robots, jellyfish, ambient flocks, image-to-ASCII, dither, and WebGL
surfaces. The scenes are dependency-free, render to a single canvas, and work
in vanilla TypeScript, React, or as custom elements.

The goal is not a novelty background. It is a composable visual layer with
real options: palettes, density, speed, reduced-motion support, pause/resume,
events, and actions.

Install: npm install ascii-things
Site: https://ascii-things.dev
Source: https://github.com/chuma-beep/ascii-things

## Show HN

Title: Show HN: ascii-things – living ASCII scenes for the web

Body:

I built ascii-things, a dependency-free library for ASCII scenes and
image-to-ASCII effects. It includes eleven scenes (lagoon, sharks, robots,
jellyfish, dog, starlings, fireflies, ants, koi, city, servers), plus image,
dither, and WebGL entries.

The scenes share a small contract for seeds, columns, palettes, speed, pause,
events, and actions. They run in vanilla TypeScript, React, or as custom
elements. Everything renders to one canvas and respects reduced motion.

Live scenes and docs: https://ascii-things.dev
Source: https://github.com/chuma-beep/ascii-things
